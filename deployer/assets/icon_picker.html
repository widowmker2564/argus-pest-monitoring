ARGUS app icon — three candidates

Each shown at 256 / 48 / 16 px on a light and a dark desktop strip. The 16 px version is a simplified ring-and-pupil (blade lines are unreadable that small). All three exist as ready multi-resolution .ico files in deployer/assets/.

A Ink square recommended

Deep-ink rounded square, silver 9-blade iris, teal pupil. Windows-11 icon language; the eye pops on any background. RECOMMENDED

B Liquid glass

The app's own light-glass identity as an icon. Softest of the three; can wash out on light desktops.

C Round badge

Full-bleed circular lens barrel. Strongest camera metaphor; circles sit less native among Win11 squares.
